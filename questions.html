Asking and booking

How do I ask about a session?

Send your name, preferred date and number of participants. We'll discuss the session details and send the payment instructions.

Prepare your message

Where can I see the latest schedule?

Check the schedule page for posted dates. If the date you want isn't listed, message us to ask about upcoming sessions.

See Upcoming Sessions

Can I ask something before choosing a date?

Of course. Send us your question and we'll help you understand the class before you decide.

Does sending a request reserve my place?

No. Your preferred date is a request. We'll confirm availability and send the payment instructions, and your place is confirmed once we've completed the booking arrangements with you.

Costs and gear

What should I check before booking?

Ask about the full cost, equipment options, venue and class requirements. We can also clarify what to bring and how the reservation works.

Is gear included in the pool class?

The advertised Introduction to Freediving class fee doesn't include gear or the pool fee. Ask us about equipment options and the current pool fee so you know your total before booking.

Are photos and videos included?

Photos and videos are listed as inclusions in the advertised Mabini trip packages. For the pool class, ask us what's available for your session.

Trips

Do you run trips outside the pool?

Yes. Freedive Clark runs fun dive trips to Mabini, Batangas, including a Sunset Yacht Dive on October 24, 2026. Freediving in Bani, Pangasinan is coming soon.

See the trips

Where are the carpool pickup points?

Carpools are booked separately from the trip packages. Pickup points depend on the trip:

  • Sunset Yacht Dive: Capas, Tarlac (Crossing), Dau Terminal and NLEX exits. PHP 2,500 round trip.
  • Fundive All-In Package: Capas, Tarlac Crossing (Chowking), Dau Terminal, Marquee Mall, SM San Fernando and NLEX exits. PHP 2,200 round trip.

Ask us which pickup suits you when you message.
